****************HEALTHCARE****************
Problem:
	Women in South Africa struggle to access reproductive health information in their local language
Solution:
	Our solution would be building an app that not only allows them to track their cycle but also get access to reproductive health info
	in their local language.They can use the AI chatbot to input their symptoms and it can give them info on their reproductive health. Chatbot
	can also map nearby donation points for free pads, and nearby clinics or hospitals.
	Languages : English, Zulu, Xhosa, Afrikaans
App name:
	uMama(Mother/nurturer): Reproductive health is a journey that women shouldn't have to walk alone. uMama is a chatbot that will hold your hand
	as you walk that journey. It will advise and teach women regarding their reproductive health. uMama will be your reproductive health mentor.
